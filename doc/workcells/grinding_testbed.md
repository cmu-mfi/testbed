# Robot Grinding Testbed

![rgt](/files/rgt.png)

The robot grinding testbed is a workcell to explore research areas of robotic grinding. A few areas of research that groups are actively working on are listed below:

* **Digitally Manufactured Fluidic Sensing For More Fluid Digital Manufacturing**\
Designs and vascularizes 3D-printed fluidic sensors within flexible lattices to enable real-time force and vibration feedback during grinding.

* **Visuo-Tactile Pretraining for Grind Surface Inspection**\
Builds a shared visuo-tactile latent space to improve vision-only defect detection, reduce tactile sensor wear, and enable continuous learning.

* **Grounded Skill Controllers and Perception for Adaptive Robot Grinding and Inspection**\
Enables robots to execute object-centric grinding skills with precision, adapting to geometric irregularities using human demonstrations.

* **Foundation Models for Fixtureless Robotic Surface Finishing**\
Creates RoboFin3D simulation and dataset alongside perception foundation models to drive bimanual, fixtureless robotic finishing systems.
